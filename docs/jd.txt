Backend / Full Stack Developer
Location: Dubai or Remote | Type: Full-Time | Experience: 3-6 years
About CrossVal
CrossVal is reimagining financial operations for businesses in the MENA region. We're building a smart financial platform that automates bookkeeping, tax compliance, reporting, and FP&A, so founders and finance teams can focus on growth, not spreadsheets. We've been recognized by Deloitte as one of the top 50 fastest-growing tech startups in the region, and we're part of the NVIDIA Inception program.


CrossVal is like Cursor for Finance. Just like Cursor lets engineers ask natural-language questions about their codebase, CrossVal lets finance teams query, understand, and act on their financial data instantly, grounded in clean, connected data from banks, accounting tools, and tax systems.
What You'll Do
You'll join a fast moving product engineering team where everyone owns real scope:


* Build and own backend services in Node.js and TypeScript on AWS
* Design NoSQL data models in MongoDB, including schemas, indexes, and query patterns
* Build and maintain integrations with third-party financial systems, covering OAuth flows, webhooks, rate limits, and inconsistent data from the other side
* Take applications end to end: build, test, deploy, monitor, back up
* Work on automation and AI agent workflows that act on live financial data, where correctness and auditability matter most
* Ship fast, with a high sense of ownership


The role is backend-first, but we work in a shared Next.js and TypeScript codebase, so you'll often carry a feature through to the UI rather than handing it off.
Must-Haves
* Strong Node.js and TypeScript
* Solid NoSQL data modelling, with proven MongoDB experience in schema and index design
* Hands-on AWS experience, and comfort operating what you deploy
* A security instinct: careful secrets handling, least-privilege access, and building auditability into systems that touch financial data
* Experience integrating third-party APIs at production quality, including auth, retries, idempotency, and failure handling
* Ability to take an application end to end: build, test, deploy, monitor, maintain
* Exposure to LLM and agent tooling in production
* Driven, detail-oriented, and quick to pick up new tools
Bonus (Nice-to-Have)
* Full-stack range: comfortable in Next.js and React, building the interface for the APIs you write
* Product sense for internal tools and dashboards, where you decide what the screen should show
* Git-based workflows and CI/CD pipelines
* Other databases (DynamoDB, Postgres)
* Other cloud platforms (GCP, Azure)
* Prior work in finance, accounting, or B2B SaaS, or comfort with concepts like double-entry, chart of accounts, and reconciliation
* Workflow automation tooling such as n8n
This Role Might Not Be For You If
* You prefer working inside an existing framework over owning a service end to end
* You'd rather hand infrastructure to a dedicated DevOps team
* You want a strict backend-only boundary and no involvement with the frontend
* You prefer detailed specs over figuring out ambiguous problems independently
* You're not curious about AI and LLM tooling
